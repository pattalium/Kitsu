# Kitsu 2.2.1

## What's new

- Adds a complete 21-creature wild encounter catalog across seven rarity tiers.
- Saves device-bound creature unlock codes securely for every paired Kitsu.
- Redeems codes through the Kitsu website and installs unlocked packs with the web flasher.
- Shows nearby owned Kitsu separately from MeshCore contacts, lets you pet them over direct Kitsu radio, and gives a first meeting its own lower wild-encounter chance.
- Retains the Bluetooth reliability fix introduced in Kitsu 2.1.6.
