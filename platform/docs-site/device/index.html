Manual/Device controls

PORTRAIT DEVICE REFERENCE

One button. Every Kitsu screen.

Kitsu uses the Heltec's PRG button for its entire local interface. A short press is less than 750 ms. A hold is 750 ms or longer.

Main menu

CONNECT
Opens the local connection menu.
FEED
Reduces hunger and records a care action.
PLAY
Gives quick companion play and improves mood.
GAMES
Opens Signal Catch, Pounce, and Back.
INBOX
Shows received MeshCore messages, newest first.
RADIO
Starts a 60-second listening activity for verified nearby adverts.
SLEEP
Puts Kitsu to sleep until the next button press or app action.
INFO
Shows device, radio, connection, and companion information.
BACK
Returns to Home.

Connection menu

BLUETOOTH
Shows the local Bluetooth state. Hold while disconnected to open the time-limited PAIR PHONE window; hold while connected to view that connection.
BACK
Returns Home. A short press moves between BLUETOOTH and BACK.

Display power saving

The OLED turns off after about two minutes of inactivity while awake and after about twenty seconds while dreaming. Press PRG to wake the display. This does not reset Kitsu and does not mean the radio receiver has stopped.

MeshCore in the background

When MeshCore is enabled, reception continues in the background. Leaving the RADIO activity stops that companion activity, but it does not disable reception and it does not automatically authorize radio transmission.

PRG is also the boot button.Do not hold PRG while resetting or connecting power unless you intentionally want the Heltec's firmware-download mode.

Portrait orientation

Kitsu's OLED interface is designed for the Heltec mounted in portrait orientation: 64 pixels wide and 128 pixels tall. All labels, menus, pairing prompts, and status screens are laid out within that canvas.
