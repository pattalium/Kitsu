Manual/Bluetooth and offline use

LOCAL-FIRST CONNECTIVITY

The phone talks directly to Kitsu.

Kitsu has no product account, gateway setup, Wi-Fi setup, or runtime-server dependency. Nearby Android control uses one authenticated Bluetooth connection. Put the phone in airplane mode, turn Bluetooth back on, and the supported app features still work.

Connect a saved Kitsu

  1. Choose the Kitsu card.

    The app can retain up to three independently authorized Kitsu records. Select the one you want to use.

  2. Tap Connect.

    The app scans for the exact bonded device, opens GATT, and proves possession of the saved controller credential through the encrypted application handshake.

  3. Wait for Connected.

    The same connection carries state, history, care actions, messages, MeshCore configuration, and firmware-update commands. It stays open until you disconnect, select another device, forget authorization, or leave Bluetooth range.

What works offline

  • Read current companion needs, battery, mood, installed pack state, history, peers, messages, and channels.
  • Pet, feed, play, start a listening period, and send an eligible direct or channel MeshCore message.
  • Enable or disable the pinned local MeshCore radio profile; the app synchronizes Kitsu's clock before time-sensitive actions.
  • Import a signed .kitsu-fw package and update the inactive firmware slot over Bluetooth.
  • Disconnect one saved Kitsu without affecting another saved authorization.
Internet permission is absent.The local-first Android release cannot silently switch to a web service, upload companion state, or depend on a server when Bluetooth fails. A Bluetooth failure stays a Bluetooth failure and the app shows the repair action.

Connection states

StateMeaning
SavedThe phone has an encrypted controller credential but is not connected.
Permission neededGrant Nearby devices, or Location on older Android versions, before scanning.
Bluetooth offTurn on the phone's Bluetooth radio; Kitsu does not need Wi-Fi or mobile data.
ConnectingThe app is scanning, opening GATT, or completing the authenticated application handshake.
ConnectedThe selected Kitsu is ready for direct local commands.
Out of rangeMove closer, wake the Heltec if needed, and tap Connect again.
Pairing requiredThe saved app credential and Android bond no longer form a valid pair; repeat the physical pairing flow.

Disconnect versus Forget authorization

Disconnect closes the active GATT session and leaves the saved authorization ready for a later Connect. Forget authorization is a security action that requires the Kitsu nearby: Android connects if needed and asks it to durably revoke this phone's controller root. Android removes its local credential after the accepted receipt, or after a lost receipt is resolved by the old credential being authoritatively rejected. The Android OS bond may remain visible, but it cannot authenticate the removed application controller.

Proximity is the ownership boundary.Approve pairing only while holding your device, compare the six-digit values exactly, and press PRG only for an action you initiated. Kitsu is deliberately reflashable and does not claim resistance against an attacker with physical access and flashing tools.
