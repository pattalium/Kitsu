Service terms

Scope

K32 Kitsu is a companion and radio service for supported Kitsu devices. It is not an emergency service, safety-of-life system, public communications carrier, or guaranteed message-delivery service.

Responsible use

Use must follow applicable radio, privacy, and computer-misuse rules. Do not use the service to harass people, interfere with networks, bypass access controls, distribute malware, or submit unlawful content. Mesh messages may be received by other radio participants and should not contain information that must remain private.

Availability and changes

The service depends on local networking, Internet access, and radio conditions. Maintenance or security incidents may interrupt access. Signed release metadata and device compatibility checks are used to prevent silent downgrade or unverified downloads.

Reports

Security, abuse, privacy, and service reports can be submitted through the web contact route.
