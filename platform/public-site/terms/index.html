PUBLIC POLICY - UPDATED 22 AUGUST 2026

Project terms

Scope

K32 Kitsu is local-first companion and radio software for supported Heltec devices. It is not an emergency service, safety-of-life system, public communications carrier, or guaranteed message-delivery service.

Responsible use

Use must follow applicable radio, privacy, and computer-misuse rules. Do not use Kitsu to harass people, interfere with radio networks, bypass access controls, distribute malware, or transmit unlawful content. Mesh messages may be received by other radio participants and should not contain information that must remain private.

Availability and changes

Nearby app controls depend on a compatible phone, Bluetooth, device power, and radio conditions—not on a Kitsu runtime service. The public download, documentation, and source sites may still be interrupted by hosting maintenance. Signed release metadata, exact hashes, device compatibility checks, and A/B rollback protect firmware installation.

Reports

Project bugs and private security-reporting links are listed on the static support page.
