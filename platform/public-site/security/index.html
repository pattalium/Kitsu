Report a security issue

How to report

Use the K32 web contact route and select Security vulnerability. Include the affected page or component, impact, reproducible steps, and a safe reply address. Do not include credentials, private keys, companion secrets, or personal radio traffic.

Safe research

Good-faith testing must use systems and devices you own or have explicit permission to test. Avoid privacy impact, denial of service, social engineering, persistence, destructive actions, and access to other people's information. Stop and report if sensitive data is encountered.

Response

The owner reviews stored open reports through the authenticated service. There is no public email address and no promise of a bounty or fixed response deadline. Confirmed issues are prioritized by exploitability and impact; public disclosure should wait until a fix or mutually agreed date.

Operational boundary

Administrative interfaces, certificate ingress, metrics, databases, and private device listeners are not public testing targets. Public testing must stay within the ordinary user-facing web and device interfaces.
