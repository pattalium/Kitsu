Privacy and retention

Who operates this service

K32 Kitsu is an independently operated companion service for enrolled Kitsu devices. Public infrastructure vendors may carry encrypted web traffic, but companion data and account services remain under the operator's control.

Data used

  • OpenID Connect identifiers and optional profile details used to authenticate the owner.
  • Companion and gateway identities, signed device events, state snapshots, peer observations, queued actions, and security audit records needed to provide remote access.
  • Short-lived session, anti-replay, rate-limit, and certificate records needed to protect the service.
  • Messages sent through the public contact form, including the reporter's reply address. Source network addresses are stored only as keyed one-way digests.

Companion secrets and private keys are not stored in plaintext. The backend stores encrypted companion secrets and public certificate material. The Android app uses Bluetooth directly when the companion is nearby.

Retention

Device events, peer history, and completed remote actions are retained for 90 days. Security audit records are retained for 365 days. Expired browser and rate-limit records are removed automatically. Open contact messages expire after 180 days; resolved messages expire after 30 days. Backups may retain already-deleted records until the relevant encrypted backup expires.

Deletion

An authenticated owner can request deletion through the companion service. A seven-day cancellation period applies. When it expires, the service removes the sign-in identity, prevents recreation from an old token, revokes certificates, cryptographically erases wrapped companion secrets, deletes events, actions, and sessions, and retains only the minimum anonymized security record required to prevent abuse.

Questions

Use the web contact route. No public mailbox is claimed.
